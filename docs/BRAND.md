# Brand

## Name

Babel references the Tower of Babel: language boundaries, reconstruction, and the ambition to make text travel without collapsing its structure.

## Positioning

Babel is a careful tool for format-preserving translation workflows. It should feel precise, calm, and serious rather than magical or gimmicky.

## Voice

- Direct and technical.
- Clear about legal and quality boundaries.
- Confident about validation and structure preservation.
- Avoid claims that imply automatic perfect translation.

## Copy Rules

Use:

- "Preserve XHTML. Translate in batches. Rebuild clean."
- "Agentic ebook translation pipeline."
- "Validation gates before packaging."

Avoid:

- "One-click perfect book translation."
- "Bypass copyright restrictions."
- "AI magic."

- Motif: stacked text blocks forming a tower.
